# Changelog

## [0.2.0]

Reading tools, the Archive and downloads for every platform. Updating keeps your papers, books
and settings.

- New book reader. It opens EPUB, PDF, MOBI, AZW3 (without copy protection), FB2 and CBZ
  comics, keeps lines to a comfortable length on mid-size screens, and shows title pages whole.
- Highlights and notes: select text, pick one of four colours, add a note. Each book has a
  notes list that jumps back to the passage. Save one book's notes, or all of them, as Markdown
  with the whole passage. Highlights stay when a book leaves the shelf.
- Find in the open book (Ctrl+F), and a filter for your shelf.
- Online book catalogues (OPDS 1 and 2): add one by its address, browse, search and download
  EPUB or PDF to your shelf. Optional login, with the password kept in the system's password
  store (app-private storage on Android) and sent only to that catalogue's own site.
- The Archive: every paper you printed, listed by date and searchable by any word in its
  stories. Opening a result shows that day's paper at that story.
- On narrow phones the top bar wraps instead of pushing buttons off-screen, and a long book
  title no longer widens the page.
- Downloads for Windows, macOS (Apple Silicon), Linux (AppImage and .deb) and Android, built by
  the release workflow with checksums. Desktop builds are not code-signed yet.

## [0.1.0]

First version: the daily paper with the evidence score, the bookshelf with public-domain
catalogues and your own EPUB files, and the self-tuning e-ink style reader. Linux and Android.
